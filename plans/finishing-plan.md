## Envelope border (approved)
1. Knot each pair of tails at row ends; trim to about 1".
2. Base round: loosely surface sl st around just inside edge, beside BS on sides: about [W] top/bottom, [R] each side (1 per row end; Samantha confirmed), ~[2W+2R] total. Join, fasten off.
3. Back flap (WS): 1 hdc in back loop of each sl st, ch 2 at corners; join. Rnd 2: hdc each st, (hdc, ch 2, hdc) in corners (+8 sts). Fasten off.
4. Front flap (RS): same, in other loop of same sl sts.
5. Join: hold flaps together, sc through both enclosing tails, 3 sc each corner.
Border adds ~1"/side: finished ~[W*4/15+2]" x [R*4/15+2]" (worsted #4, H 5mm, 15x15 = 4").
Sources: ashleeslint.com/2020/07/22/envelope-border-for-mosaic-crochet/, stardustgoldcrochet.com/easy-envelope-border-tutorial-for-mosaic-crochet/, juniperandoakes.com/blog/howtocrochet-hdc-envelope-border-overlaymosaiccrochet/

## Pillow: back panel ch [W], rows to match [R]; join WS together with sc, 3 sc corners; insert form before closing.
## Wall hanging: rod pocket ~6 rows sc across top, fold & sew; or evenly spaced tabs.
