The Yarn Side · Overlay mosaic designer

Everything runs in your browser. Worked flat, bottom up, every row right to left.

Start from a picture

Upload pixel art, check the grid, and pick a letter for each color. Then tap Start designing. You only do this once per picture; everything else happens in Design. To open a saved piece or a board instead, go to Combine.

Check how your picture was read

The pink lines should fall between the squares of your art, one box per square, with each dot inside a single color. If they drift, change the counts and tap Re-read.

Colors → letters

Give every color a letter: A background, B contrast, C / D extra strands carried along the row and swapped in where they show. Tap a color to see where it is in the picture. The picture keeps its own colors until you tap Show letters.
